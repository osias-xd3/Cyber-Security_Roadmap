How To Use This Roadmap: The Parallel Loop

Cybersecurity skill-building is not a strict linear line — it is three parallel engines running continuously at increasing intensity. Don't wait to "finish" networking before touching Linux, and don't wait to "finish" studying before doing your first TryHackMe lab.

01

1. Learn

Foundations, protocols, architecture, video courses, RFCs, and guided theory.

02

2. Practice

Hands-on labs, OverTheWire wargames, TryHackMe rooms, Hack The Box, and your virtual home lab.

03

3. Prove

Verified free certs → foundational paid cert (Security+) → public GitHub & LinkedIn portfolio write-ups.
